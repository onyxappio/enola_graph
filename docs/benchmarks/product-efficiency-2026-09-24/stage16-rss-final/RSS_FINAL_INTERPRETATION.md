# Stage16 RSS follow-up: interpretation plan before final pair

The original three-pair structural RSS result remains evidence: candidate median
648,331,264 versus baseline604,864,512 bytes, with non-overlapping ranges. Two
later complete pairs differ by about +0.08% and -2.34%; neither supersedes the
original cohort. Candidate6 has no paired baseline and must stay unpaired.

The scheduled pair7 must retain full evidence regardless of its direction.
Report original cohort, follow-up complete pairs and the unpaired arm separately.
A pooled descriptive summary may be supplemental, explicitly naming all included
pairs; it is not a statistical proof that a regression disappeared. A single
favorable final pair cannot close the memory gate by itself. Sampling variability
must be distinguished from retained heap, total allocated bytes and memory
footprint, none of which is interchangeable with process maximum RSS.

Recheck all per-arm correctness, quiet-hold and competitor receipts before using
any latency or RSS row. If the deadline leaves an incomplete pair, preserve it
without substituting a result from an earlier hold. Report uncertainty rather
than extending the slot or selectively repeating the slower arm.

Stage17 is a separate candidate on a separate baseline. Its allocation reduction
must not be used to justify Stage16 RSS or combined-stage performance without
measuring the actual integrated binary.
